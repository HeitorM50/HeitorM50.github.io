Privacy Policy — Omarchy Calendar

Last updated: 25 August 2026

Omarchy Calendar is an open-source desktop widget for the Omarchy Linux desktop. It shows your upcoming Google Calendar events in the status bar and notifies you before they start.

Who runs this application

There is no service and no server. The application runs entirely on the computer of the person who installs it, using an OAuth client that person creates in their own Google Cloud project. The author does not operate any infrastructure that this application talks to, and has no access to any user's data.

What data is accessed

With the calendar.readonly scope, and only after you explicitly authorize it, the application reads upcoming events from your primary Google Calendar: title, start and end time, location, description, conference link, and reminder settings.

The scope is read-only. The application cannot create, modify, or delete anything in your calendar.

Where data is stored

All data stays on your own machine, in files readable only by your user account (permission mode 0600):

  • ~/.local/state/omarchy/io.github.heitorm50.gcal/credentials.json — your OAuth client ID, client secret, and refresh token.
  • ~/.local/state/omarchy/io.github.heitorm50.gcal/events.json — a cache of your upcoming events, so the widget works offline.
  • $XDG_RUNTIME_DIR/omarchy-gcal/ — a short-lived access token and a record of reminders already shown, cleared when you log out.

Where data is sent

Nowhere except Google. The application makes requests to oauth2.googleapis.com to refresh its access token and to www.googleapis.com to read your calendar. It contains no analytics, no telemetry, no crash reporting, and no third-party services of any kind. Your calendar data is never transmitted to the author or to anyone else.

Data retention and deletion

The event cache is overwritten on every sync and holds only the next few days. To remove everything the application has stored, delete its state directory:

rm -rf ~/.local/state/omarchy/io.github.heitorm50.gcal

To revoke the application's access to your Google account, remove it at myaccount.google.com/permissions. Because the OAuth client belongs to you, you can also delete the entire project from your Google Cloud console.

Children

This application is a developer tool and is not directed at children.
